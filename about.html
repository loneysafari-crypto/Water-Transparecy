About the Project

Lake Rukwa Monitor is an advanced environmental monitoring platform that utilizes Google Earth Engine to track changes in Lake Rukwa, one of Tanzania’s important wetlands.

Our Mission

We aim to provide accurate, real-time, and accessible satellite-based data to support sustainable management of Lake Rukwa’s water resources, biodiversity, and surrounding ecosystems.

Project Objectives

  • Develop a web-based system for computing and monitoring water transparency in Lake Rukwa using remote sensing.
  • Integrate Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and geospatial analysis.
  • Provide interactive visualization and analysis tools for users.
  • Support evidence-based decision making for water resource management.

Technology Used

This project is powered by Google Earth Engine, using multi-temporal satellite imagery from Landsat, Sentinel-1 & Sentinel-2.

Scientific Background

Water transparency is a key indicator of aquatic ecosystem health as it directly affects light penetration, photosynthesis, and overall water quality.

In Lake Rukwa, water transparency has been declining due to increased sediment inflow, nutrient enrichment, and human activities such as deforestation, agriculture expansion, and small-scale mining in the catchment area.

This project addresses these challenges by developing a modern web-based system that integrates remote sensing and geospatial technologies to provide near real-time information on water transparency.
